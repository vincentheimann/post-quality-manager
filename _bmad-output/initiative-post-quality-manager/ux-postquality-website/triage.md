This triage covers every finding in the three reviews: `review-rubric.md`, `review-accessibility.md` and `review-honesty-conversion.md`. The full findings are in `validation-report.md`.

**How to read it:**

- **Source ids:**
  - Accessibility and honesty ids are the reviewers' own.
  - The rubric gave no ids, so its findings are numbered by severity in the order they appear (rubric H1–H2, M1–M6, L1–L14), as in `validation-report.md`.
- **Memlog entries** are cited by their line number in `.memlog.md`, for example memlog #31.
- **Merged rows:** a row that merges findings from several reviewers takes the highest severity among them.
- **Settled items:** "(settled)" marks a resolution the session already decided before triage.

- **Findings:** 84 in the reviews, 70 after deduplication.
