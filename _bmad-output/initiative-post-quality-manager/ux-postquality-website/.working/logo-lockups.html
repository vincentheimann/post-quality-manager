Logo lockup options

Six ways to set the Post Quality Manager icon beside its name, for postquality.com and the files around it. These are options to react to: nothing here is decided, and the picks go into docs/brand.md once you have made them.

Fixed, not up for choice: the icon from story 51. Square navy tile, white Q, green check rising to the right. It is never mirrored, stretched, recoloured or given rounded corners, and it never gets a third colour. Every option below uses the same icon artwork without changes.

1. Variants

The large preview uses a 56 px tile (36 px on a phone). The three cells below each preview use the header size, a 32 px tile, on white, on the WP-admin grey and on navy. On navy the lockup sits on the lighter band that section 2 recommends.

When it fits: the favicon, social avatars, the plugin icon files (128, 256 and 512 px), the Freemius product image, and any spot too small for a wordmark (tile under 20 px, or too narrow for the name).

2. The icon on navy

The tile is navy #1b2a41. Put it on a #1b2a41 surface and the square disappears. The fixes below leave the icon's colours alone.

Recommendation: S2, the lighter navy band. The icon artwork stays identical everywhere: site, favicon, plugin files, Freemius. No keyline has to be redrawn for each size, and the square still holds at 16 to 20 px, where a 1 px keyline weighs as much as the Q itself. The cost is one extra site colour, #33496b, used only behind the lockup on dark sections. The rule: the tile never sits directly on #1b2a41. Keep S1 as the fallback for a dark surface you cannot recolour, and use it only from a 48 px tile upward.

3. Specs

T is the tile's side, and every measurement is a multiple of it. The figure shows A with a 96 px tile (32 px on a phone).

clear space 0.5 T on every side
T Post Quality Manager cap height = ring Ø = 0.44 T 0.375 T
  • ProportionWordmark size 0.63 T (sans) or 0.66 T (serif), which puts its cap height at about 0.44 T, the outer diameter of the Q's ring. The caps line up with the top and bottom of the ring and are centred on the tile. Weights: A and D 600; B and E 600 + 400.
  • Gap icon → text0.375 T: 12 px at a 32 px tile, 10.5 px at 28, 7.5 px at 20 (round to whole pixels). C keeps the same gap, sets the name at 0.46 T and "Manager" at 0.31 T, with 0.17 T between the lines.
  • Clear space0.5 T on all four sides of the lockup's outer box: no text, edge or other mark inside it. In practice, a 32 px tile in a header 64 px tall (2 T) fills the bar exactly. The icon alone (F) keeps 0.25 T where a platform does not add its own padding.
  • Sizes in useHeader 32 px (desktop), 28 px (phone). Footer 20 px. 404 page icon 64 px. Sharing card 96 px. Favicon 16 and 32 px (icon.svg). Plugin and Freemius files 128, 256 and 512 px.
  • Minimum sizesOne-line wordmarks (A, B, D, E): 20 px tile, which puts the text at 12.6 px. Stacked C: 40 px tile. Below a 20 px tile, or where the lockup does not fit the width (A needs about 240 px at a 32 px tile), the wordmark drops and only F remains, down to 16 px.
  • Final artworkConvert the wordmark to SVG outlines, so it no longer depends on the visitor's fonts and the cap alignment holds on every system. Source typefaces under the open SIL OFL 1.1 licence: Inter for A to D, Source Serif 4 for E. The live text on this page uses system fonts only.

The favicon is icon.svg with icon-128x128.png as fallback (story 85), so the browser draws it at 16 px (32 device pixels on a high-density screen). On a dark tab strip the tile edge fades (1.11:1 on #202124), but the white Q and green check stay clear. The favicon file cannot carry the band, and adding a keyline would change the artwork, so that edge is accepted. Checked at 12× enlargement: the tile edges stay sharp at 16, 20 and 32 px. At 16 px on a standard-density screen the Q's ring (about 1.3 px wide) is soft but still reads as a Q with a check; at 20 px and up, and at any size on a high-density screen, it is clean. A pixel-fitted 16 px PNG would sharpen the low-density case, but it is optional and would be a new file, not a change to the icon.

Social sharing card, og:image 1200 × 630

The card has no screenshot and no third-party mark. Build it once as a static PNG (GitHub Pages does no image processing) and point every page's og:image at it. Once the real plugin captures exist, a second version could show the Dashboard on the right.

5. Your picks

  1. Wordmark: A (plain full name) or B (short form stressed). E only if the site goes serif.
  2. Phone header: keep the full name down to 360 px, or switch to D whenever a button joins the header.
  3. Is C needed? Only if a square spot comes up (the 404 page, a sidebar). Otherwise drop it and keep four variants.
  4. Icon on navy: S2, the band #33496b (recommended), or S1, the keyline.
  5. Sharing card: light or dark.
  6. Outline source: Inter for the sans wordmark (Source Serif 4 if E). The chosen lockup then goes into docs/brand.md as SVG files plus the specs from section 3.
