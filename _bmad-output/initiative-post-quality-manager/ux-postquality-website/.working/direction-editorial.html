Direction: Editorial

The writer’s desk

Design annotation for the bmad-ux pass on postquality.com (story 57). An option to react to, not a decision.

Rationale

Post Quality Manager is sold to people who write, so the site is set like a well-made publication: serif headlines, a reading column, ink on warm paper, navy as the ink accent. The plugin appears as captioned figures in an article, so the Dashboard does the persuading while the page stays calm and credible.

One primary action sits above the fold, and the prerequisites sit in the margin beside it, so nobody installs and then bounces (user path A2).

Honest nudges used

  • Prerequisites up front: a margin note in the hero, then the full “What you need” block straight after Figure 1, before anything install-like.
  • “The free plugin is the trial”: low-commitment start, in the hero and on the Free plan.
  • You hold the keys: posts go only to your own n8n and Anthropic; the author receives nothing; this site sets no cookies.
  • Risk reversal on Pro: the 14-day money-back guarantee and “nothing deleted if Pro lapses”, side by side.
  • Show, don’t claim: real Dashboard labels with fictional demo posts, labelled as demo content in every caption.

Not used: countdowns, scarcity, testimonials, ratings, user counts or cost figures we don’t have.

How it works

You bring n8n and the key. The plugin brings the dashboard, the workflow and the agents.

  1. 1

    Connect your accounts

    The setup guide asks for three values: your n8n address, an n8n API key and your Anthropic key. It sets the workflow up for you and tests the connection both ways.

  2. 2

    Send posts for review

    Review one post or select several. Your site sends each one to your own n8n workflow, which asks Anthropic’s models.

  3. 3

    Fix what matters first

    The result comes back to WP Admin: a score, issues, suggested edits and takeaways. Fix next puts the lowest scores at the top.

Your site→Your own n8n→Anthropic’s models→Back in WP AdminThe author of the plugin receives nothing from your site.
