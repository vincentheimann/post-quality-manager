Direction: Plain and honest

The skeptic's page: postquality.com home, desktop first.

Written for the evaluator who doubts they can run this: user path A2 says the need for an own n8n, an AI key and an unknown AI cost is where many evaluators bounce, so the page answers that in the hero, before it asks for anything. It reads like good documentation (text first, a table of contents, small technical facts in monospace, one navy button) and earns trust through what it admits rather than what it claims.

Honest nudges used (pins in the mock)

  1. 1Prerequisites stated up front: own n8n, an Anthropic key, you pay Anthropic per review, WordPress 6.6+ / PHP 8.0+, all before the first button (A2).
  2. 2Plain status: "pre-release, not installable yet", and the button says what it does (opens an email, subject filled in).
  3. 3"The free plugin is the trial."
  4. 414-day money-back guarantee, set next to the price.
  5. 5Nothing is deleted if Pro lapses.
  6. 6You hold the keys: content goes only to your n8n and, through it, to Anthropic; this site sets no cookies and runs no analytics.

Left out on purpose: urgency, scarcity, social proof (there is none yet), AI-cost figures (a worked example is coming).

How it works

your site→ your n8n workflow→ Anthropic's models→ back to WP Admin
  1. Install and open the setup guide. It asks for three values: your n8n address, an n8n API key and your Anthropic key.
  2. The guide sets the workflow up in your n8n and tests the connection both ways.
  3. Review a post. Your site sends it to your own n8n workflow, which asks Anthropic's models for the review.
  4. Read the result in WP Admin: a score, the issues, suggested edits and key takeaways.
  5. Follow the trend on the Dashboard: the average score over time and the posts to fix next.

You bring n8n and the key; the plugin brings the dashboard, the workflow and the agents. Prefer to set it up by hand? The documentation covers that path too.

Four pillars, one score

Every review scores the same four pillars: Structure, Readability, Engagement and Credibility. You tailor them to your niche through a Blog Profile and Editorial Standards, never code.

The Dashboard

One screen in WP Admin for the whole blog: the average score, how many posts sit in each status, the five posts to fix next and the trend over eight weeks.

Questions before you start

What will the AI cost me?

You pay Anthropic per review, on your own key; the Free and Pro prices cover the plugin only. A quota and a token limit cap the spend. A worked example with real figures is coming.

Where does my content go?

Only to your own n8n and, through it, to Anthropic. The author receives nothing from your site unless you opt in or buy Pro; the Privacy policy lists what that covers. Your Anthropic key is checked once and never stored by the plugin.

What if I stop paying for Pro?

Pro features lock and the site returns to Free. Nothing is deleted: your reviews, settings, schedules and role rights stay, and renewing unlocks them again.
